A little help, when you need it.

Questions about your plans, Reminders, or the app? Start here.

Updated 27 September 2026

Get in touch

Email tappspro@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, operating-system version, Dailora version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot can help—please hide personal or sensitive content first.

Start with one Task

Use “+ Task” to capture something you want to do. Inbox holds Tasks you haven’t organized yet. When you’re ready, place them in a Pillar or Goal.

Pillars and Goals

A Pillar is an ongoing part of your life, such as Home or Work. A Goal is an outcome you want to reach. Tasks can live directly in a Pillar or inside a Goal.

Schedule, Deadline, and Reminder

Schedule chooses when a Task becomes part of your plan. Deadline records the date it needs to be finished. A Reminder requests a local alert at a particular time. They are separate controls.

Where is my data?

Your planning content is stored on the device. Dailora has no account or cloud-sync service. It does not currently offer an in-app export or backup feature. Be careful before uninstalling the app or clearing its storage; device backup and restore behavior depends on your operating system.

A Reminder didn’t arrive

Check Dailora’s notification permission in your phone settings, then check the Task’s Reminder time. Focus, Do Not Disturb, and device power settings can affect how alerts appear. Dailora’s Settings shows the notification-permission status.

Manage crash reporting

Open Settings → Crash reports. New installs start with reporting on. You can switch it off while continuing to use Dailora. Re-enabling it after turning it off in the same session takes effect on the next launch.

Something isn’t working

Close and reopen the app, then check whether a newer version is available in your store. If the issue continues, contact support. Avoid clearing app storage as a first troubleshooting step because your plans are stored locally.
