Privacy policy

A clear account of what stays on your device, what diagnostic reports may contain, and the choices you have.

Effective 27 September 2026

Your plans stay in app-local storage

Dailora stores your Tasks, notes, Pillars, Goals, Checklist Items, Tags, and planning dates in local app storage. Preferences are also stored locally. Dailora does not provide an account, cloud sync, or its own cloud backup service. Your device or operating system may have separate backup settings.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, Dailora asks your device to schedule local Reminders. Notification content may appear on your lock screen according to your device settings. You can manage notification permission in your device’s settings.

Crash reports

Dailora uses Sentry to help diagnose crashes and technical errors. Crash reporting is on for new installs. You can turn it off under Settings → Crash reports and continue using the app. Turning it off stops new capture; reports already queued may still be sent while reporting closes. If you turn it back on in the same session, it takes effect the next time you open Dailora.

What reports can contain

Reports can include technical information such as error types, stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device information, timestamps, and event identifiers. Dailora’s JavaScript reporting filters out Task titles, notes, and error-message text. Native crash reports use the native SDK’s format and can contain additional technical crash details. Dailora does not use session replay, advertising, or product-usage analytics.

Service providers and retention

Sentry processes diagnostic reports for us. Its hosted service retains error events for no more than 90 days on standard plans; the period for our account may be shorter. We do not keep a separate copy of these reports. Sentry may receive your IP address when a report is sent, even though Dailora does not add your name or an app account identifier. See Sentry’s retention information.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete your planning content using the app’s available controls. Removing the app removes its local app storage, subject to any device backup or restore behavior. Turning off crash reports does not delete reports already received by Sentry. To request deletion of a report, email tappspro@gmail.com with the approximate date, device, and what happened. We will try to locate and delete matching reports. Because Dailora has no account identifier, we may be unable to identify a particular report as yours; we will tell you if that is the case.

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and attachments. Please avoid sending sensitive Task content. Support email is handled through Google Gmail. We keep messages while needed to respond and maintain a record of the issue, then delete them when no longer needed. You can request deletion of your support messages at any time, subject to any legal obligation to keep them.

Audience and changes

Dailora is a general personal-productivity app and does not ask users for their age. If you believe a child has sent us information through a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. We will update this page when our practices change and show the effective date above.
